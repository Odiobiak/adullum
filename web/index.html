Before you talk out loud

This voice is an AI, not a person or clergy. It can get things wrong or miss real distress, and it's no substitute for a pastor, a counselor, or emergency care. In crisis, reach a licensed professional, or in the US call or text 988.

What you say goes to third-party speech providers, and this call isn't confidential the way a talk with clergy would be. Continuing means you accept that.

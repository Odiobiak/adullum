Privacy

Last updated 7 November 2026

People bring very personal things to Adullum: prayers, grief, marriages, money, doubts. This page says plainly what happens to what you write here. Adullum is run by [operator name], who is responsible for your data. Questions go to hello@adullum.app.

The short version

  • What you keep here is yours. Nobody else using Adullum can see it, and every request is checked on our server to be yours.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, and we never sell or share your data for marketing.
  • Aquila is an AI. To answer you, what you write to her is sent to OpenAI, our AI provider.
  • You can download everything, or delete your account and everything in it, from Settings at any time.

What we keep

  • Your account: your email, the name Aquila calls you, your settings and time zone. Your password passes through our server to our sign-in provider and is never stored by us.
  • What you make here: conversations with Aquila, your journal and reflections, prayers and fasts, notes, highlights and saved verses, reading plans, sermon notes and their photos, churches you add, goals and habits, declarations, and anything you choose to keep from Refuge.
  • What Aquila remembers: short notes about you, taken from conversations, your journal and your prayer list, so she can know you over time. You can see, remove or switch these off in Settings. They are never treated as Scripture.
  • Reminders: if you turn on notifications, the address your browser gives us for sending them.
  • On your device: your sign-in, theme and a few preferences are kept in your browser's storage, with a copy of today's home screen so it opens at once. Signing out clears your sign-in and that copy. We don't use tracking cookies.

Refuge

  • Write it out: "Let it go" never leaves your device. Only "Keep it" stores anything.
  • Talk with Aquila: the talk is held in your browser and gone when you leave, unless you press Keep. Aquila's replies are written by OpenAI, so what you say is sent there to answer you. These talks are never recorded in our quality logs and never become memory notes.
  • Amens: nobody using Adullum, the people who look after the wall included, is ever shown who wrote a post. A post is stored with your account so you can see, manage and delete it. Before it goes up it's checked automatically (using OpenAI's moderation service) and may be read by a person on our admin desk. Posts leave the wall after a set time.
  • If something you write suggests you may be in danger, Adullum shows you helplines. It never contacts anyone on your behalf.

Who else handles your data

We use a small number of services to run Adullum. Each handles only what it needs.

  • Supabase: our database, sign-in, sign-in emails and photo storage.
  • Render: runs our server.
  • OpenAI: writes Aquila's replies and checks Amens posts. It receives what you ask or write for those features, along with relevant notes Aquila keeps about you. Under OpenAI's API terms this isn't used to train their models, and it may be kept by them for up to 30 days to detect abuse.
  • Langfuse: keeps a record of Aquila's answers outside Refuge (the question, the answer and the steps between), so we can find and fix mistakes. These records aren't labelled with your email and are deleted after [retention period].
  • Cognigy and its speech providers: only if you use "Talk out loud". Your voice and its transcript go to them to hold the call. The call isn't confidential the way a talk with clergy would be.
  • Google: serves the fonts this site uses (which shares your IP address with Google), and handles sign-in if you choose "Continue with Google".
  • Cloudflare (cdnjs): serves the code that draws Connections.
  • YouTube: plays sermons in Listen when you press play, using its privacy-enhanced player.
  • Stripe: takes gifts if you give. Your card details go to Stripe, never to us.
  • Apple, Google or Mozilla: deliver notifications to your device, if you turn them on.

Some of these providers process data outside your country, including in the United States. [Describe the transfer safeguards that apply, for example the providers' standard contractual clauses.]

Why we use it

We use your data only to give you Adullum: to answer you, keep what you keep, send the reminders you ask for, keep the Amens wall safe, and fix what goes wrong. [Legal basis, for example: performing our agreement with you, and your consent for optional features such as memory and notifications.] We never use it for advertising or to build a profile for anyone else.

Your choices

  • Download your data: Settings, then Account. You get one file with everything you keep here.
  • Delete your account: Settings, then Account. This removes everything at once and can't be undone. Copies in our database provider's backups expire within [backup window], and records held by OpenAI and Langfuse expire on the schedules above.
  • Memory: switch it off, or remove any note, in Settings.
  • Notifications: turn them off from any bell, or in your browser. Signing out stops them on that device.
  • You can also ask us to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it, at hello@adullum.app. [If you are in the UK or EU, you can complain to your data protection authority.]

Keeping it safe

Everything travels over encrypted connections. The keys to our database stay on our server and never reach your browser. Every request for your data is checked to be yours, and only the few people named to look after the Amens wall can open the admin desk, which never shows who wrote a post. If something ever goes wrong that puts your data at risk, we will tell you and, where the law requires it, the authorities.

What you write

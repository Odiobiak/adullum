# Welcome message for beta testers

Paste into an email or a group chat. Change the bits in angle brackets.

---

Hi <name>,

Thank you for helping test Adullam before anyone else sees it. It's a dwelling place for your walk with God: somewhere to study the Bible, pray, rest and journal, free and with no ads. Alongside you is Aquila, a study companion who answers from Scripture and gives the chapter and verse for everything she quotes, so you can always check her.

Open **https://www.adullamapp.com** and create an account. On a phone, add it to your Home Screen (on iPhone: Share, then "Add to Home Screen") so it opens like an app and can send you reminders.

Three things to try:

1. **Ask Aquila something you've wondered about**, like "Why did Jesus speak in parables?" or "What does Psalm 23 say about fear?" Then tap a reference under her answer to see where it connects.
2. **Open the Cave and look around Refuge.** Write something out, try Be still, or share a prayer request on Amens. Nobody sees your name, and others can pray for you.
3. **Add a prayer to your list**, then tap "Pray with Aquila" or "Declare over it".

Found something broken, confusing or lovely? Tap your initials at the top, then **Send feedback**. It opens an email that already says which page you were on. Or write to hello@adullamapp.com. A screenshot helps a lot.

It's early, so some things will be rough. Everything you keep is private to you, and you can download or delete it all from Settings.

Thank you,
<your names>
